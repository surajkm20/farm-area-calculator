How to get a location

  1. Choose what you're recording above: Farm boundary, Plantation or Borewell.
  2. Stand on the spot and open Google Maps. Wait until the blue dot stops moving.
  3. Press and hold your finger on the blue dot. A red pin drops there.
  4. The numbers appear in the search bar at the top, like 12.523000, 76.897000. Tap them to copy.
  5. Come back here, paste, and tap the green button.
  6. For the farm and the plantation, walk to the next corner and repeat. Go around in order, all the way back to the first corner. For the borewell, one point is enough.

Or tap Use phone GPS while standing on the spot. It reads the GPS for 15 seconds and averages it. Tap again to add the point sooner. This works when the page is opened in Chrome at surajkm20.github.io/farm-area-calculator, not inside the Claude app.

How accurate is this?

Phone GPS is usually off by 3 to 5 metres at each point. For a farm of an acre or more, the total area is usually close, often within a few guntas.

Under coconut or arecanut trees, near hills, or next to buildings, GPS is weaker. Stand still for 20 to 30 seconds at each point before adding it.

This is for your own planning. For sale, partition or a boundary dispute, get an official survey through the Bhoomi survey department.

Karnataka land units

1 acre = 40 guntas. 1 gunta = 16 aane = 1,089 sq ft (about 101 sq m).

Your RTC (Pahani) writes area as acres-guntas-aane, for example 2-12-6 means 2 acres, 12 guntas and 6 aane.

1 acre = 100 cents = 43,560 sq ft = 0.4047 hectare.
