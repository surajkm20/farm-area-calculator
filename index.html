    How to get a corner's location

    1. Stand on the corner of your farm and open Google Maps. Wait until the blue dot stops moving.
    2. Press and hold your finger on the blue dot. A red pin drops there.
    3. The numbers appear in the search bar at the top, like 12.523000, 76.897000. Tap them to copy.
    4. Come back here, paste, and tap Add corner.
    5. Walk to the next corner and repeat. Go around the farm in order, all the way back to the first corner.

    The Use phone GPS button may not work inside the Claude app. Google Maps always works.

    How accurate is this?

    Phone GPS is usually off by 3 to 5 metres at each corner. For a farm of an acre or more, the total area is usually close, often within a few guntas.

    Under coconut or arecanut trees, near hills, or next to buildings, GPS is weaker. Stand still for 20 to 30 seconds at each corner before copying the location.

    This is for your own planning. For sale, partition or a boundary dispute, get an official survey through the Bhoomi survey department.

    Karnataka land units

    1 acre = 40 guntas. 1 gunta = 16 aane = 1,089 sq ft (about 101 sq m).

    Your RTC (Pahani) writes area as acres-guntas-aane, for example 2-12-6 means 2 acres, 12 guntas and 6 aane.

    1 acre = 100 cents = 43,560 sq ft = 0.4047 hectare.
